# Starcode mark

The symbol combines code brackets with a central four-point star. The brackets
refer to integration; the star is the recognizable center. The open shape keeps
the mark readable in Shopware's small extension icon. Dark ink and emerald-teal
separate the parts without adding a background badge.

Use the transparent PNG at `src/Resources/config/plugin.png`. Keep equal clear
space around it, preserve its aspect ratio and pair it with the plain word
"Starcode" when the name is needed. Do not add a shield, tagline or shadow.

The design follows the Phoenix approach by giving each part a purpose and
checking the combined mark in its real interface. Golden-ratio relationships
informed the brief; no mathematical construction or superiority is claimed.

Generated with the built-in image tool on 29 October 2026, then refined with the
same tool. It is a raster master, not an editable vector. No third-party brand
assets were supplied. Trademark availability has not been assessed.

## Generation brief

One compact geometric symbol for Starcode, a self-hosted Shopware form-protection
plugin. Fuse inward-facing code brackets and a four-point star through negative
space. Optically balanced, clear at small sizes, near-black and emerald-teal,
equal transparent margins. No lettering, badge, shield, lock, gradients, 3D,
shadows or literal phoenix. The refinement requested the same geometry with
uniform solid fills and a transparent background.

The retained PNG is the selected output. Requested rendering and export size
are design directions, not a claim of pixel-perfect color or vector precision.
